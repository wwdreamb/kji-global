What counts as “qualified”?

A researched prospect matches agreed criteria based on available information. A reply means someone has responded. An interested lead has expressed relevant interest. We report these stages separately, with sources and notes, so you can see what each result means.

A few things to know

Do we need to book a call?

No. Send your brief by email. We handle questions, scope, approvals, updates, and delivery in writing. Review and reply when it works for you.

What results can I expect?

You receive the deliverables agreed in your scope: researched prospects, outreach drafts, or support with approved email follow-up. Responses and new clients depend on the market and offer; we do not guarantee sales or revenue.

Do you provide bookkeeping or financial advice?

This offer covers lead research and email outreach support. Bookkeeping, accounting, tax, legal, and financial services remain with independent providers.

What information do you need to start?

Your firm’s website, business contact details, target industries and geography, services offered, and capacity for new clients. We do not need client books, banking details, tax returns, or passwords.

What does the pilot cost?

Email your requirements and we’ll send a written scope and fee. Work starts only after approval. Sending an inquiry does not commit you to a purchase.
